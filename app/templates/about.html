About CinePhile — HCI & ML Transparency

CinePhile is an intelligent movie discovery application integrating Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) usability principles with content-based machine learning and information retrieval ranking. Developed by Ayush Sabale.

2. Model Architecture & Methodology

  1. Multi-Attribute Composite Document: Rather than relying solely on plot summaries, films are weighted by domain discriminative power: genres (×3), director (×3), keywords (×2), cast (×2), and overview (×1, stemmed).
  2. Zero Data Leakage: Vocabulary and inverse document frequencies (IDF) are strictly fitted on the 77.8% training split. Test titles are projected into the vector space at inference time.
  3. L2-Normalized Cosine Similarity: Pairwise dot products compute semantic alignment in vector space.
  4. IMDB Bayesian Confidence Blending: Combines cosine similarity (88%) with Bayesian weighted rating (12%, threshold $m=50$ votes) to balance content similarity with verified community consensus.
  5. Dynamic Profile Centroid: Watchlist recommendations aggregate bookmarked films using an exponential recency decay ($\lambda = 0.15$).
